Prove the fact. Not the file.

A shop needs to know one thing: is this person over 18. A bank needs to know that the name on the form matches a verified identity. Today, answering either question means handing over a whole ID card that gets photographed and kept. SabiID is a broker that sits between the identity sources and the businesses that check identity. It asks the source a single yes or no question, signs the answer, writes one line to a tamper-evident log, and passes back the answer alone. Nobody builds a new copy of your record, so there is no new database to steal.
